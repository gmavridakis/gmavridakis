I build and lead the delivery of backend-heavy systems, mostly Java and Spring on Kubernetes with Angular or Flutter in front, for public-sector and enterprise clients. I started in tech in 2017 on a service desk, built Magento e-shops, spent three years at Scorpio Group delivering Java, Angular and Azure solutions, and joined European Dynamics in 2022 to engineer a large EU public-sector platform. Since 2025 I lead a team there, owning technical analysis, architecture decisions, effort estimation and the evaluation of new technologies.

My end goal is to become a software architect. I hold the iSAQB CPSA-F and I am deliberately widening my range: a second bachelor's degree in Electrical & Computer Engineering at NTUA alongside full-time work, an MIT course on LLM-based agents, and a habit of turning whatever I learn into small reusable pieces. Learning a new technology and then finding where it fits in a real system is the part of this job I enjoy most.
